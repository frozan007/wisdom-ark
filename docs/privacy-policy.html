Wisdom Ark Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

Wisdom Ark is a tuition management app for tutors. It is published by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app stores, why, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

Who the app is for

Wisdom Ark is meant for tutors and tuition centre staff, who are adults. It is not directed at children, and students do not use the app or have accounts in it. Tutors use it to keep records about their own students.

Information we store

Your account. When you sign up we store your name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us in readable form.

Your profile. Optionally, your tuition name, phone number, address and a profile photo.

Records you enter about your students. Wisdom Ark stores the information you choose to enter, which can include:

  • student name, date of birth, gender, school, class, subjects and a photo
  • parent or guardian name, phone numbers, email address and home address
  • monthly fee, payment due day, joining date and your own notes
  • class schedules, homework, follow-ups and other notes
  • fee payments: amount, date, month, payment method (cash or UPI), reference number, remarks and, for UPI payments, a screenshot you upload as proof

You are responsible for having the right to store this information, for example by getting consent from a parent or guardian, and for keeping it accurate.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the app for you: to show your records, calculate fees and pending payments, build reports, and send password reset emails you request. We do not use it for advertising, we do not build profiles about you or your students, and we do not sell it.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Your data is stored with Supabase, our cloud database and file storage provider, which processes it on our behalf. Records are tied to your account and are only shown to you when you are signed in. All data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

We do not share your data with any other third party, except where the law requires us to.

Camera, photos and files

  • Camera: used only when you tap "Take photo" to add a student photo, profile photo or payment screenshot.
  • Photos: the system photo picker opens only when you tap "Choose from gallery". The app sees only the photo you pick.
  • Files: when you export a PDF or Excel report, the file is saved temporarily on your device so you can share or save it with an app you choose.

Analytics and advertising

Wisdom Ark contains no ads, no advertising trackers and no analytics SDKs.

How long we keep data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete a student or a record in the app, it is removed from our database. Archived students stay in your account until you delete them.

Deleting your account

To delete your account and all data stored with it, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Wisdom Ark account".

We will delete your account, profile, student records, schedules, notes, payments and uploaded photos within 30 days, and confirm by email. Backups are cleared on their normal cycle after that.

Your rights

You can view, correct and delete your records at any time in the app. For anything you cannot do in the app, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
